OUR PROGRAMS

CPR

CPR stands for cardiopulmonary resuscitation. It is a life-saving emergency procedure performed when someone's breathing or heartbeat has stopped, such as after a heart attack, drowning, or cardiac arrest.

FDI X BFA

What is BFA?

The primary medical care and immediate assistance given to someone who gets suddenly injured or falls ill before professional emergency services can take over. The main goals are to save the person's life, stop their condition from getting worse, and help them start recovering. It includes simple but vital actions that anyone can learn, such as recognizing when an environment is unsafe, checking if a person is breathing, or stopping severe bleeding with a bandage. You do not need any special medical tools or previous medical knowledge to perform basic first aid because it uses everyday items and straightforward techniques, like chest compressions for a stopped heart or the Heimlich maneuver for someone choking on food. By taking quick and calm action during those crucial first few minutes of a medical crisis, an ordinary bystander can keep a person stable and safe until an ambulance or doctor arrives at the scene.

THE PEOPLE BEHIND FDI

FDI is built by a team of young people working across leadership, operations, organization, education, media, and marketing. Each department plays a different role, but all contribute toward the same goal: making practical health education more accessible, engaging, and valuable to the communities we serve. Behind every workshop, partnership, campaign, and educational initiative is a team responsible for turning ideas into real impact. Meet the people and departments working together to build and develop the Future Doctor Initiative.
